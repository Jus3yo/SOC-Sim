# CLD-0204: Internal port sweep outside scan window: 203.0.113.44

Engagement: Cloudora · Shift 1 · 2026-08-25
Verdict: False positive · Severity (my rating): Informational (reported Medium)
QA review: Excellent

## Alert
Rule 'TCP port sweep' fired caused by host 203.0113.44 (weekly vulnerability scan) probing 3,800+ TCP ports across 160 internal hosts between 23:30 at 01:1 UTC Sunday night into Monday.

## Hypothesis
Malicious: attacker could have hijacked the vulnerability scan during the new scanner schedule for this week for port sweep reconnaissance.
Benign: Legitimate scan, but 'TCP port sweep' rule fired due to new schedule change from Sunday - Monday from Monday - Tuesday.

## Evidence checked
Morning handover ( LDN-SCAN-01 (203.0.113.44) was moved forward to Sunday 23:00 through Monday 03:00 UTC this week only, to clear the window for storage migration work. Announced by it-ops Friday. Change window CHG-2101.);
Alert fired a window that was between 23:30 and 01:1x UTC Sunday night into Monday;
Traffic pattern: sequential SYN probes, no data transfer.

## Action
Closed referencing CHG-2101. Noted for future authorized scanner schedule changes that scans that are not in routine times generate noise.

## Justification
CHG-2101 references a scanning window change to Sunday 23:00 through Monday 03:00 UTC this week. The alert window lasted between between 23:30 and 01:1x UTC Sunday night into Monday. The alert window falls into the authorized scanning window change. Traffic pattern also indicates no malicious actions took place due to traffic being sequential SYN probes and no data transfer (routine for a vulnerability scan).

## Tuning note
Something that could help without blinding the soc is adjusting the rule to recognize approved and authorized change windows
