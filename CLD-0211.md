# CLD-0211: Periodic outbound connections: MAN-WS-211 to cdn-metrics.example

Engagement: Cloudora · Shift 3 · 2026-08-27
Verdict: False positive · Severity (my rating): Informational (reported Medium)
QA review: Excellent

## Alert
Rule 'Beaconing pattern' fired on endpoint MAN-WS-211 (user priya.nair) due to automated outbound TLS connections reaching out to cdn-metrics.example (198.18.140.9) at strict 60-second intervals with uniform payload sizes starting at 07:00 UTC. The behavior triggered the automated C2 detection logic.

## Hypothesis
Malicious: MAN-WS-211 was compromised by malware using periodic 60-second TLS beacons
Benign: Outbound connections are legitimate agent heartbeat telemetry from the newly rolled out Manchester office endpoint pilot.

## Evidence checked
Helpdesk ticket HD-5144 verifies the endpoint monitoring pilot for Manchester went live this week, explicitly stating agents transmit heartbeats to cdn-metrics.example every 60 seconds;
Morning handover confirms the scheduled Manchester pilot under HD-5144;
proxy_egress_week1.log records consistent ~412–414 byte POST requests from MAN-WS-211 to 198.18.140.9 on 60-second intervals spanning Oct 6 (09:00:00Z) through Oct 7 (07:59:00Z).

## Action
Closed referencing HD-5144. Noted to tune the beaconing rule for known agent domains to avoid generating repetitive false-positive noise during the pilot.

## Justification
HD-5144 and shift notes explicitly authorize the Manchester workstation agent pilot and define its expected beaconing frequency (60s) to cdn-metrics.example. Telemetry from MAN-WS-211 completely matches this expected heartbeat pattern in packet size, method, and cadence, confirming benign administrative activity rather than unauthorized traffic.


## Tuning note
Adjust beaconing alert specifically for destination cdn-metrics.example on Manchester subnet hosts during the pilot window.
