# CLD-0207: Outbound mail surge: marketing@cloudora.io

Engagement: Cloudora · Shift 2 · 2026-08-26
Verdict: False positive · Severity (my rating): Informational (reported Medium)
QA review: Excellent

## Alert
Rule 'Outbound send rate' fired on marketing@cloudora.io. 640 external messages sent between 09:10 and 09:40 UTC via the connector for mailflow-platform.example. Content is uniform, October product newsletter. Send originated from the marketing platform's IP range, not a user session.

## Hypothesis
Malicious: account compromised or connector abused to mass-send spam or phishing at volume. Benign: legitimate scheduled marketing newsletter send, volume matches an expected campaign.


## Evidence checked
 helpdesk_week1.csv, HD-5138: October newsletter scheduled Tuesday 09:10 via mailflow-platform.example, approximately 640 recipients; send timestamp matches HD-5138's scheduled time exactly; content is uniform, one newsletter, not varied lure content; origin is the marketing platform's own IP range, not a compromised user session or unrecognized device.


## Action
Closed referencing HD-5138. Noted for future outbound rate alerts that scheduled marketing sends should be checked against helpdesk before triage.

## Justification
HD-5138 references the October newsletter scheduled for Tuesday 09:10 via mailflow-platform.example, approximately 640 recipients. The send matches that exactly: right time, right recipient count, right platform. It is one newsletter, not the mixed or personalized content typical of a large scale phishing attack. Traffic pattern also indicates no compromise took place due to the send originating from the marketing platform's own IP range, not a user account or session that could have been hijacked.
